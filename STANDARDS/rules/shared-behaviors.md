# Shared behaviors

These patterns apply across skills. Prefer them over restating the same rules in every skill.

- When missing or conflicting information could change the outcome, briefly explain why. Ask the needed questions together in a concise numbered list, each with a short recommended answer and its key tradeoff. Do not restart settled discovery or require an interview for straightforward work.
- Preserve unrelated work, unique contributions, and existing authorization boundaries.
- Keep changes, planning, and verification proportional to the request. Stop at the agreed finish line.
- Flag inaccessible references rather than inventing their contents.
- Report what was done or decided, what was verified, and any remaining limitations or gaps.
- Do not expand the agreed objective with side questions, optional ideas, or unsolicited features.
